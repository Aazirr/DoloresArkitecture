## Design intent

Villa Mactan sits on a narrow coastal lot where the prevailing northeast wind and afternoon sun dictated every decision. The building is oriented to capture the breeze through a series of louvered screens on the east façade, while deep cantilevered overhangs shade the western glass walls from direct afternoon glare.

## Materials

Exposed board-formed concrete makes up the primary structural walls, chosen for its thermal mass and its honest expression of how the building is put together. Local *lauan* timber lines the ceilings of the living areas, adding warmth and acoustic softness to the otherwise mineral palette.

## Landscape integration

The pool extends the living floor plane toward the shoreline, dissolving the boundary between interior and exterior. A planted berm on the street side provides privacy without closing the house off from the neighbourhood.
