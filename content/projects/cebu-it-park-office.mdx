## Brief

The client — a fast-growing technology firm — needed a workplace that could support focused individual work, spontaneous team collaboration, and formal client-facing meeting rooms, all within a single open floor plate.

## Zoning strategy

Rather than the conventional open-plan grid, the floor is organised as a gradient: quiet focus zones on the perimeter capture natural light through full-height glazing, while a central "collision corridor" connects the café, library, and informal meeting pods. The enclosed rooms anchor the corners with full acoustic separation.

## Material palette

Acoustic felt panels in muted earth tones line the collaboration pods, reducing reverberation without resorting to foam. White-oak veneered casework threads through the space as a unifying material, appearing as shelving, partitions, and desk surfaces.
