## Scope

Commercial projects demand that architecture do more than look good — the space must support the specific workflows and culture of the organisation while remaining flexible enough to evolve. We bring both spatial thinking and a working knowledge of building regulations, accessibility requirements, and operational logistics.

## Services include

- Office fit-outs and workplace design
- Retail and showroom interiors
- Hospitality — cafés, restaurants, and boutique hotels
- Mixed-use building design

## Process

Commercial projects move faster than residential ones. We offer expedited design phases and can work alongside your project manager or PMC if one is in place. Early engagement with the contractor is encouraged to keep the schedule realistic.
