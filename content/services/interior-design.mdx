## Integrated or standalone

Interior design services are available as part of a full architectural commission or as a standalone engagement for completed buildings. In both cases we approach the interior as a continuation of the architectural idea, not a decorative layer applied at the end.

## What it covers

- Space planning and furniture layout
- Material and finish specifications (floors, walls, ceilings)
- Lighting design — natural and artificial
- Custom joinery and built-in furniture
- Loose furniture, fixtures, and art curation
- Contractor coordination and site inspections

## Our sensibility

We favour durable natural materials — timber, stone, concrete, rattan — over trend-driven finishes. The goal is an interior that ages gracefully and improves with use, rather than one that looks perfect in photographs but feels hollow in person.
