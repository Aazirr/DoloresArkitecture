## Our approach

Every home we design begins with a listening phase. We want to understand how a family actually lives — morning routines, how often guests stay, whether the children do homework at the kitchen table — before we pick up a pencil.

From that understanding we develop a spatial brief: which rooms need to be connected, which need separation, where natural light matters most, and how the house relates to its site and neighbourhood.

## What we deliver

- **Schematic design** — massing, floor plans, key sections, and a 3D model or renders so you can understand the proposal spatially before committing.
- **Design development** — detailed drawings, material selections, and coordination with structural and MEP engineers.
- **Construction documents** — permit-ready drawings and specifications.
- **Construction administration** — site visits and contractor coordination throughout the build.

## Typical timelines

A custom home from first meeting to permit-ready drawings typically takes 6–10 months, depending on scope and regulatory complexity. Construction follows separately and is handled by your chosen contractor.
