# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective residential, commercial, institutional, and interior-design clients in Cebu and across the Philippines. Visitors are evaluating the studio's design judgment, credibility, and fit before starting an architectural project.

## Product Purpose

D.ARK+ is a portfolio-led marketing website for Dolores Arkitecture. It presents the studio's work, services, process, and point of view; lets visitors explore selected projects through interactive 3D models; and converts qualified interest into direct project inquiries.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, and assured. The experience should feel like entering a working architecture studio after hours: controlled light, measured linework, tactile drawings, and quiet confidence.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic luxury-architecture templates, soft rounded card grids, decorative glassmorphism, and empty minimalism. Do not copy the supplied CHORUS reference literally; translate its technical grids, connective lines, condensed typography, and high-contrast pacing into an architectural drafting language unique to D.ARK+.

## Design Principles

- Let the work and the drawing process carry the story.
- Make every line structural: grids, rules, dimensions, and annotations should organize real content.
- Use motion like a camera move: deliberate, smooth, and focused on one moment at a time.
- Keep the path from first impression to project inquiry direct and legible.
- Preserve performance and clarity on mobile connections without flattening the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA with semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, sufficient text contrast, generous touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives. Interactive 3D and graphic treatments must always degrade to understandable static content.
